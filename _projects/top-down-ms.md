Proteoforms are the specific form of proteins after considering all possible variations that can occur via gene mutation, alternative splicing, and post-translational modification. These are the molecular entities that interact with other proteins and ligands, propagate signals, and ultimately carry out biological processes.

The widely practiced bottom-up approach digests proteins into peptides, breaking the linkage between individual modification sites. The top-down approach, in which intact proteins are ionized and then fragmented in the gas phase, can provide the overall extent and location of modifications.
