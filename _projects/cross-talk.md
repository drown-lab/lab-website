Precision medicine is founded on therapies that selectively engage proteins in diseased tissue while avoiding healthy tissue. Drugs like imatinib and vemurafenib exploit changes in ligand binding sites modified by mutation, but this does not fully exploit the differential expression of proteoforms in disease states.

We are developing improved methods for measuring proteoform-specific reactivity and exploring new chemical matter that can achieve selective inhibition.
